# Access Your Homeowner Portal

Register for secure online access to your account, statements, and payments through the resident portal operated by the Association's managing agent, Progressive Association Management.

## How to register

1. Go to the resident portal at [progressiveam.cincwebaxis.com](https://progressiveam.cincwebaxis.com).
2. Click Sign In, then Create Account.
3. Enter your property information.
4. The management office reviews and approves your request.
5. You will receive an email with a link to set your password. It expires in 5 days, so act promptly. Check your junk mail.
6. Log in with your email and new password.

## More than one property

After your first property is approved, log in, open Account Info then My Profile, and scroll to Register an Additional Property. Once verified, you can switch between properties.
