# Violation Report Form

This page reproduces the substantive text of the Violation Report Form attached to the Guidelines. Blank fill-in fields on the printed form (names, unit numbers, phone numbers, dates and signatures) are not reproduced. To obtain and submit the current form, contact management through the homeowner portal.

Source: Neighborhood Guidelines (Residential Handbook), Tribeca at Central Park West Association, adopted by the Board July 24, 2018.

---

There must be at least two (2) signatures from residents of Tribeca to pursue a violation against another Owner or tenant. Please provide a brief description of the violation and be as specific as possible with enough detail so that the Board can take the appropriate actions required under the circumstances. Camera dated pictures are helpful, if applicable. Please note that as the reporting party, you may be called as a witness at a hearing before the Board and the alleged violating member may be given the opportunity to examine this report or ask questions of you as part of his or her due process rights. Your signature on this Violation Report indicates your willingness to be present at a hearing for purposes of determining whether a violation has occurred.

The form collects the following information:

**Report Filed By:** the printed name, unit number, phone number, date and signature of each of the two reporting residents.

**Alleged Violator Information:** the alleged violator's name (if known) and unit number (if known); a description of the alleged violation (with space to continue on the reverse side of the form); the date(s) and approximate time(s) the alleged violation has occurred or occurs; and how often the alleged violation has occurred as of the date the Violation Report is being filled out.

The form carries the following notice:

> THE ALLEGED VIOLATOR MAY HAVE A RIGHT TO A COPY OF THIS REPORT. THE ASSOCIATION DOES NOT GUARANTEE THAT THIS REPORT WILL REMAIN CONFIDENTIAL.
