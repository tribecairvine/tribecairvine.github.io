# Recycling

What goes in your recycling cart, and what to keep out. These guidelines are from the community's waste hauler.

**Keep recyclables loose in your cart.** Do not bag them. Bagged recyclables are mistaken as trash on the sorting line. (This is the opposite of the food-scrap carts, where you should bag and tie your scraps.)

## Always recycle

Empty and dry: plastic bottles and containers, food and beverage cans, paper, flattened cardboard and paperboard, and glass bottles and containers.

<figure style="margin:12px 0"><img src="/assets/images/wm-recycle-yes.jpg" alt="Always Recycle: empty and dry plastic bottles and containers, cans, paper, flattened cardboard, glass" style="max-width:100%;border-radius:8px;border:1px solid #e4e2da"></figure>

## Do not include

No food or liquid, no foam cups or containers (put those in the trash), no loose plastic bags or film, no clothing, furniture, or carpet, and no hazardous waste or batteries.

<figure style="margin:12px 0"><img src="/assets/images/wm-recycle-no.jpg" alt="Do not include in recycling: food or liquid, foam, plastic bags, clothing and furniture, hazardous waste or batteries" style="max-width:100%;border-radius:8px;border:1px solid #e4e2da"></figure>

For food scraps, see the [Food Scrap (Organics) Program](food-compost.html).
