# Pests & Insects: Who Handles What

A plain-language guide to pest responsibility. This is a convenience summary; the CC&Rs control.

Source: CC&Rs Article 2, Sections 2.1.2 and 2.1.3(e).

## Wood-destroying pests (termites and similar organisms)

Under CC&Rs Section 2.1.3(e), the Board may adopt an inspection and prevention program for wood-destroying pests and organisms if it determines the program is economically feasible.

- **If the Association has adopted such a program,** the Association runs it. To treat an infestation, the Association may require Owners and occupants to temporarily vacate the Unit on no less than 15 and no more than 30 days' notice. Occupants are responsible for their own accommodations during the relocation. Any damage caused by the Association's entry is repaired by the Association, and the program costs are a Common Expense.
- **If the Association has not adopted a program,** then under CC&Rs Section 2.1.2(a) a wood-destroying pest prevention program is the responsibility of each Owner.

To confirm whether a program is currently in place, contact management.

## Other pests inside your Unit

Under CC&Rs Section 2.1.2(a), each Owner must keep the Unit in a clean and sanitary condition. General pest issues inside a Unit, that are not wood-destroying organisms covered by an Association program, are part of the Owner's maintenance obligation.

## If an Owner does not act

Under CC&Rs Section 2.1.3(e), the Association may, but is not required to, perform corrective janitorial, landscaping, and repair work in a Condominium if the Owner fails to do so, after Notice and Hearing.

## Community pest control

The Association's common-area pest-control service is currently on a monthly cycle, addressing rodent and insect activity community-wide.

The CC&Rs, especially Sections 2.1.2 and 2.1.3(e), control.
