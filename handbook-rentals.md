# Rental of Condominiums

This page reproduces the Neighborhood Guidelines rules governing rental and leasing of Units.

Source: Neighborhood Guidelines (Residential Handbook), Tribeca at Central Park West Association, adopted by the Board July 24, 2018.

---

**1. Rental.** An Owner shall be entitled to rent the Owner's Unit (but not a portion thereof) for a term of not less than sixty (60) days. The Owner shall be responsible for all actions of the tenant. (CC&R's Section 2.9)

**2. Rental Agreement.** Any rental or leasing agreement shall be in writing, shall provide that the lease or rental is subject to the Governing Documents and shall provide that any failure to comply with any provision of the Governing Documents shall be a default under the terms of the rental agreement. Landlord/Tenant Package forms must be obtained from The Manager, completed and returned to the Manager before tenant has access to Neighborhood Association Property via fobs or gate entry access. Landlord/Tenant Package may require prior review by the Neighborhood Association Board.

**3. Compliance with Governing Documents.** A copy of the Governing Documents and these Guidelines shall be provided by the Owner to each tenant. The leasing Owner shall, at all times, be responsible for their tenant's or tenant's compliance with all of the provisions of the Neighborhood Declaration and Guidelines pursuant to the occupancy and use of the Unit.

**4. Timesharing.** No Owner may physically or legally subdivide his Unit in any manner, including dividing such Owner's Unit into time-share estates or time-share uses. (CC&R's Section 2.10)

**5. Assessments and Voting Rights.** A tenant shall have no obligation to the Neighborhood Association to pay assessments imposed by the Neighborhood Association nor shall any lessee have any voting rights in the Neighborhood Association.
