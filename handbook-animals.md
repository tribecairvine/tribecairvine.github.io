# Animals

This page reproduces the Neighborhood Guidelines rules governing pets and other animals kept at Tribeca.

Source: Neighborhood Guidelines (Residential Handbook), Tribeca at Central Park West Association, adopted by the Board July 24, 2018.

---

**1.** All residents must comply with City and County laws and regulations with regard to control and health of pets. All dogs shall have a current license and all dogs and cats shall have an identification tag. Loose, unattended dogs, cats or other animals without a license or identification tag may be reported to the local Animal Control for pickup.

**2.** Each resident may have a maximum of two (2) dogs or two (2) cats or a combination thereof but not to exceed a maximum of two (2). Fish in aquariums holding no more than thirty (30) gallons of water, small birds inside birdcages, and small domestic reptiles and rodents kept within appropriate small-sized enclosures may also be kept as household pets, so long as they do not result in unreasonable annoyance and are not obnoxious to other residents. No livestock or poultry shall be kept, maintained or bred in any Unit or elsewhere within Tribeca.

**3.** Pets must be kept in a container or on a leash held by a person capable of controlling the animal in any Neighborhood Association Property at all times. Pets must be under the resident's control when outside living enclosures.

**4.** Pets are not allowed to defecate or urinate on Exclusive Use Area balconies or elevators. Fecal waste deposits made by pets on any Neighborhood Association Property, including landscaped areas, sidewalks and court yard and elevators, must be promptly cleaned up by the Owner of the pet. Waste must be put in a tightly sealed plastic bag before being disposed of. Any damage caused by a pet shall be repaired/replaced at the pet Owner's expense.

**5.** Animals shall not be bathed, at any time, within any Neighborhood Association Property or Exclusive Use Area balconies. Mobile pet washing service is not allowed at Tribeca.

**6.** Pets shall not be tied to trees or any exterior building structures.

**7.** Pets must not be left outside unattended on Exclusive Use Area balconies. Pets must be kept within the Unit when the resident is away or cannot attend to them. Barking dogs on Exclusive Use Area balconies, or inside a Unit, or any other excessively noisy animal that disturbs the quiet enjoyment of any other residents will not be tolerated.

**8.** Each person bringing or keeping a pet in Tribeca shall be liable to other residents and their guests for any damage to persons or property caused by any pet brought upon or kept within Tribeca by such person or by members of his/her family or guests.

**9.** If any Owner's or other resident's animal has exhibited aggressive behavior (e.g., has attacked or attempted to attack any person or a common domesticated household animal in the Neighborhood) that animal is subject to removal from the Neighborhood in accordance with City animal control regulations and/or the Neighborhood Association's Rules and Regulations.

**10.** If, after Notice and a Hearing, the Board finds that a pet is dangerous or creates a nuisance, the Board may require the pet to be removed within seven (7) days.

**11.** Structures for the housing or confinement of any bird or other animal must not be visible from neighboring Units or the Neighborhood Association Property.

**12.** Service animals, such as seeing-eye dogs, are exempt from rules that interfere with their duties. Notice of any exemption with proof of need claimed by a resident must be delivered to The Manager upon placement of the service animal within the Unit.

**13.** No animals may be kept, bred or raised within Central Park West for commercial purposes.
