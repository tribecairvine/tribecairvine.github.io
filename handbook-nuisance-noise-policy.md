# Nuisance and Noise Policy

This page reproduces the Neighborhood Association's Nuisance and Noise Policy.

Source: Neighborhood Guidelines (Residential Handbook), Tribeca at Central Park West Association, adopted by the Board July 24, 2018.

---

In accordance with the Neighborhood Declaration, no resident may cause or permit to be caused anything which may become a nuisance or cause unreasonable disturbance or annoyance to other residents. The Neighborhood Association has adopted the following policy as a reasonable interpretation of that restriction.

**1. Horn, Whistles, Bells.** No horns, whistles, bells or other sound devices, except security devices approved by the Board may be used in any Unit.

**2. Speakers.** No sound system, loudspeakers, entertainment systems or other internal sound, or noise generating or amplifying device may be installed in any wall or ceiling.

**3. Musical Instruments.** No resident may play or permit to be played any musical instrument in such a way as to unreasonably (i) disturb other residents, or (ii) be heard between the hours of 10:00 p.m. and the following 8:00 a.m. in such a way to disturb other residents.

**4. Radio, TV, Stereo.** No resident may play or permit to be played any radio, television, stereo or similar device in such a way as to unreasonably (i) disturb other residents, or (ii) be heard by other residents between the hours of 10:00 p.m. and the following 8:00 a.m.

**6. Disputes.** Because nuisance noise is largely subjective, the Neighborhood Association cannot involve itself in every dispute which may arise between two owners. As a matter of practicality and as a benefit to its membership as a whole, the Board has adopted the following standard for determining when the Neighborhood Association will become involved in such disputes:

- **Multiple Units.** If the noise is such that it disturbs more than one unit, the Neighborhood Association will take appropriate action to abate the nuisance if the affected residents request in writing that action be taken by the Board.
- **Single Unit.** If the noise is such that it only disturbs a single unit, then the disturbance is not sufficient to cause intervention by the Neighborhood Association and the two parties will be encouraged to resolve their dispute by Alternative Dispute Resolution.

## Editorial note on source numbering

The source document is reproduced as printed. The numbered items above run 1, 2, 3, 4, then 6. There is no item numbered "5" in the recorded document, and the numbering has not been altered.
