## Owner responsibilities (recurring)

| Task | How often | Why | Source |
|---|---|---|---|

| Inspect supply lines and hoses | Annually | Catch age-related failures early | [What to Do If You See Water](water-emergency.html) |
| Service major appliances (furnace, HVAC, A/C, water heater) | Regularly / annually | Prevents leaks and mold | Residential Handbook (mold prevention) |
