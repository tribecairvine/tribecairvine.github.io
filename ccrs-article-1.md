# Article 1 — Definitions and Interpretation

This article defines the terms used throughout the CC&Rs and explains how the document is to be interpreted.

Source: Neighborhood Declaration of Covenants, Conditions, Restrictions and Reservation of Easements for Tribeca at Central Park West, recorded August 14, 2017 as Instrument 2017000341565, Orange County Official Records.

---

## 1.1 Definitions

Unless otherwise expressly provided, the following words and phrases when used in this Neighborhood Declaration have the following meanings.

### 1.1.1 Annexable Territory

Annexable Territory means the real property described in Exhibit B which may be made subject to this Neighborhood Declaration pursuant to Article 16. Any references in this Neighborhood Declaration to Annexable Territory are references to the Annexable Territory as a whole and to portions thereof.

### 1.1.2 Annual Assessment

Annual Assessment means a charge levied against the Owners and their Condominiums representing their share of Common Expenses. The Annual Assessment is a regular assessment as described in California Civil Code Section 5600(a).

### 1.1.3 Architectural Review Committee or Committee

Architectural Review Committee or Committee means the Architectural Review Committee created in accordance with Article 5.

### 1.1.4 Articles

Articles means the Articles of Incorporation of the Neighborhood Association currently in effect.

### 1.1.5 Assessment

Assessment means any Annual Assessment, Capital Improvement Assessment, Reconstruction Assessment and Special Assessment.

### 1.1.6 Board or Board of Directors

Board or Board of Directors means the Neighborhood Association's Board of Directors.

### 1.1.7 BRE

BRE means the California Bureau of Real Estate and any department or agency of the California state government which succeeds to BRE's functions.

### 1.1.8 Budget

Budget means a written, itemized estimate of the Neighborhood Association's income and Common Expenses prepared pursuant to the Bylaws.

### 1.1.9 Bylaws

Bylaws means the Bylaws of the Neighborhood Association as currently in effect.

### 1.1.10 California Non-Profit Mutual Benefit Corporation Law

California Non-Profit Mutual Benefit Corporation Law means Title 1, Division 2, Part 3 (commencing at Section 7110) of the California Corporations Code, and any successor statutes.

### 1.1.11 Capital Improvement Assessment

Capital Improvement Assessment means a charge against the Owners and their Condominiums representing their share of the Neighborhood Association's cost for installing or constructing capital Improvements on the Neighborhood Association Property. Capital Improvements Assessments shall be levied in the same proportion as Annual Assessments. Capital Improvement Assessments are special assessments as described in California Civil Code Section 5600(a).

### 1.1.12 CID Act

CID Act means the Davis-Stirling Common Interest Development Act and its successor provisions at California Civil Code Sections 4000 to 6150, or to subsequently enacted replacement statutes.

### 1.1.13 City

City means the City of Irvine, California, and its various departments and agencies.
