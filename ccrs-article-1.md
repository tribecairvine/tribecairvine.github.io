# Article 1 — Definitions and Interpretation

This article defines the terms used throughout the CC&Rs and explains how the document is to be interpreted.

Source: Neighborhood Declaration of Covenants, Conditions, Restrictions and Reservation of Easements for Tribeca at Central Park West, recorded August 14, 2017 as Instrument 2017000341565, Orange County Official Records.

---

## 1.1 Definitions

Unless otherwise expressly provided, the following words and phrases when used in this Neighborhood Declaration have the following meanings.

### 1.1.1 Annexable Territory

Annexable Territory means the real property described in Exhibit B which may be made subject to this Neighborhood Declaration pursuant to Article 16. Any references in this Neighborhood Declaration to Annexable Territory are references to the Annexable Territory as a whole and to portions thereof.

### 1.1.2 Annual Assessment

Annual Assessment means a charge levied against the Owners and their Condominiums representing their share of Common Expenses. The Annual Assessment is a regular assessment as described in California Civil Code Section 5600(a).

### 1.1.3 Architectural Review Committee or Committee

Architectural Review Committee or Committee means the Architectural Review Committee created in accordance with Article 5.

### 1.1.4 Articles

Articles means the Articles of Incorporation of the Neighborhood Association currently in effect.

### 1.1.5 Assessment

Assessment means any Annual Assessment, Capital Improvement Assessment, Reconstruction Assessment and Special Assessment.

### 1.1.6 Board or Board of Directors

Board or Board of Directors means the Neighborhood Association's Board of Directors.

### 1.1.7 BRE

BRE means the California Bureau of Real Estate and any department or agency of the California state government which succeeds to BRE's functions.

### 1.1.8 Budget

Budget means a written, itemized estimate of the Neighborhood Association's income and Common Expenses prepared pursuant to the Bylaws.

### 1.1.9 Bylaws

Bylaws means the Bylaws of the Neighborhood Association as currently in effect.

### 1.1.10 California Non-Profit Mutual Benefit Corporation Law

California Non-Profit Mutual Benefit Corporation Law means Title 1, Division 2, Part 3 (commencing at Section 7110) of the California Corporations Code, and any successor statutes.

### 1.1.11 Capital Improvement Assessment

Capital Improvement Assessment means a charge against the Owners and their Condominiums representing their share of the Neighborhood Association's cost for installing or constructing capital Improvements on the Neighborhood Association Property. Capital Improvements Assessments shall be levied in the same proportion as Annual Assessments. Capital Improvement Assessments are special assessments as described in California Civil Code Section 5600(a).

### 1.1.12 CID Act

CID Act means the Davis-Stirling Common Interest Development Act and its successor provisions at California Civil Code Sections 4000 to 6150, or to subsequently enacted replacement statutes.

### 1.1.13 City

City means the City of Irvine, California, and its various departments, divisions, employees and representatives.

### 1.1.14 Close of Escrow

Close of Escrow means the date on which a deed is Recorded conveying a Condominium pursuant to a transaction requiring the issuance of a Public Report.

### 1.1.15 Common Area

Common Area means the volumes of airspace described in the Condominium Plan for each Phase, which shall be owned by the Owners of the Units in the Phase as tenants-in-common. Any references in this Neighborhood Declaration to Common Area are references to the Common Area as a whole and to portions thereof. The Common Area in each Phase constitutes the "undivided interest-in-common in a portion of the real property," in accordance with California Civil Code Section 4125(c).

### 1.1.16 Common Expenses

Common Expenses means those expenses for which the Neighborhood Association is responsible under this Neighborhood Declaration including the actual and estimated costs of and reserves for maintaining, managing and operating the Neighborhood Association Property. Common Expenses also include unpaid Special Assessments, Reconstruction Assessments and Capital Improvement Assessments. Common Expenses include commonly metered charges for the Neighborhood (including water supplied to residential fire sprinklers), alarm monitoring services, annual backflow valve inspections, trash collection and removal (as applicable), managing and administering the Neighborhood Association, including compensation paid by the Neighborhood Association to off-site Managers, accountants, attorneys and employees, gardening and other services benefiting the Neighborhood Association Property, all Neighborhood Association Property fire, casualty, liability, worker's compensation, errors and omissions director, officer and agent liability insurance, and other insurance covering the Neighborhood and the Directors, officers and agents of the Neighborhood Association, bonding the members of the Board; taxes paid by the Neighborhood Association, amounts paid by the Neighborhood Association for discharge of any lien or encumbrance levied against the Neighborhood; maintenance required by this Neighborhood Declaration and all other expenses incurred by the Neighborhood Association for the Neighborhood, for the common benefit of the Owners.

### 1.1.17 Community

The Community means that master planned development (as defined in Section 2792.32 of Title 10 of the California Code of Regulations), of which the Neighborhood is a part.

### 1.1.18 Community Association

Community Association means the Central Park West Community Association, a California nonprofit corporation (formed pursuant to the California Non-Profit Mutual Benefit Corporation Law), its successors and assigns. The Community Association is an "association" as defined in California Civil Code Section 4080.

### 1.1.19 Community Association Governing Documents

Community Association Governing Documents means the Master Declaration, the Articles of Incorporation and the Bylaws for the Community Association, the Community Guidelines (as defined in the Master Declaration), the Design Guidelines (as defined in the Master Declaration) and any Supplemental Declaration recorded pursuant to Article 16 of the Master Declaration.

### 1.1.20 Condominium

Condominium means an estate in real property as defined in California Civil Code Section 4125. A Condominium consists of an undivided fee simple ownership interest in the Common Area in a Phase, together with a separate ownership interest in fee in a Unit and all easements appurtenant thereto. Subject to the provisions of Article 10, the undivided fee simple interest in the Common Area in a Phase is appurtenant to each Unit in such Phase and is a fraction having one (1) as its numerator and the number of Units in that Phase as its denominator. Such Common Area shall be held by the Owners of Condominiums in that Phase as tenants-in-common.

### 1.1.21 Condominium Plan

Condominium Plan means the Recorded plan, as currently in effect, for all or a portion of a Phase consisting of (a) a description or survey map of the Phase or portion thereof, which shall refer to or show monumentation on the ground, (b) a three-dimensional description of the Phase or portion thereof, one or more dimensions of which may extend for an indefinite distance upwards or downwards in sufficient detail to identify the Neighborhood Association Property, Common Area and each Unit, and (c) a certificate consenting to the Recordation thereof signed and acknowledged by the record owner of fee title to the Phase or portion thereof, and by either the trustee or the Mortgagee of each Recorded Mortgage encumbering the Phase or portion thereof.

### 1.1.22 Construction Claims Statute

Construction Claims Statute means Division 2, Part 2, Title 7 (commencing with Section 895) of the California Civil Code.

### 1.1.23 Cost Sharing Agreement

Cost Sharing Agreement means that certain Declaration of Easements and Cost Sharing Agreement between Declarant and the Rockefeller Association, Recorded pursuant to the provisions of California Civil Code Sections 1468 and 845, to (a) reimburse the Neighborhood Association for shared expenses related to maintenance of certain streets necessary for access to Rockefeller as well as certain mailbox clusters benefitting both Rockefeller and the Neighborhood, (b) create certain easements, restrictions and rights appurtenant to Rockefeller, (c) establish a working relationship between the Neighborhood Association and the Rockefeller Association, and (d) provide for other matters in connection with the interface between the Neighborhood and Rockefeller.

### 1.1.24 County

County means Orange County, California, and its various departments, divisions, employees and representatives.

### 1.1.25 Declarant

Declarant means LENNAR HOMES OF CALIFORNIA, INC., a California corporation, its successors, and any Person to which it shall have assigned any of its rights under this Neighborhood Declaration by an express written assignment. As used in this Section, "successor" means a Person who acquires Declarant or substantially all of Declarant's assets by sale, merger, reverse merger, consolidation, sale of stock or assets, operation of law or otherwise. Unless otherwise expressly provided in the Neighborhood Declaration, all actions that may be taken by Declarant may be chosen by Declarant in its sole discretion. Declarant is a "builder" as described in California Civil Code Section 6000, and a "Neighborhood Builder" as defined in the Master Declaration.

### 1.1.26 Design Guidelines

Design Guidelines mean the rules or guidelines setting forth procedures and standards for submission of plans for Architectural Review Committee approval.

### 1.1.27 Exclusive Use Area

Exclusive Use Area means the Neighborhood Association Property over which exclusive easements are reserved for the benefit of specified Owners including for balconies, stairwells and internal and external telephone wiring designed to serve a single Unit but located outside the boundaries of that Unit (as originally installed and assigned to the Unit), all as applicable to the particular Phase and all in accordance with Civil Code Section 4145. Except for internal and external telephone wiring, the approximate locations of the Exclusive Use Area balconies and stairwells in each Phase are shown on the Condominium Plan for that Phase, however, the as-built location of such Exclusive Use Areas shall control. Declarant may reserve additional Exclusive Use Areas in one or more Supplemental Neighborhood Declarations.

### 1.1.28 FHA

FHA means the Federal Housing Administration of the United States Department of Housing and Urban Development, and its successors.

### 1.1.29 FHA Requirements

FHA Requirements means the requirements applicable to the Neighborhood as set forth in (a) Mortgagee Letter 2012-18 dated September 13, 2012 (Temporary Approval Provisions for Federal Housing Administration Condominium Project Approval Process), as extended by Mortgagee Letter 2014-17 dated August 29, 2014, which amend in part Mortgagee Letter 2011-22 dated June 30, 2011 and the Condominium Project Approval Guidelines attached thereto, and (b) the U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development (HUD) Condominium Regulations of the National Housing Act, Title 24 CFR Part 203, all as updated, amended, restated, revised or replaced from time to time.

### 1.1.30 FHFA

FHFA means the Federal Housing Finance Agency, established pursuant to the Housing and Economic Recovery Act of 2008.

### 1.1.31 Family

Family means natural individuals, related or not, who live as a single household in a Residence.

### 1.1.32 Fannie Mae

Fannie Mae means the Federal National Mortgage Association, a government-sponsored private corporation established pursuant to Title VIII of the Housing and Urban Development Act of 1968, and its successors.

### 1.1.33 First Mortgage

First Mortgage means a Mortgage with first priority over other Mortgages on a Condominium.

### 1.1.34 First Mortgagee

First Mortgagee means the Mortgagee of a First Mortgage.

### 1.1.35 Fiscal Year

Fiscal Year means the fiscal accounting and reporting period of the Neighborhood Association.

### 1.1.36 Freddie Mac

Freddie Mac means the Federal Home Loan Mortgage Corporation created by Title II of the Emergency Home Finance Act of 1970, and its successors.

### 1.1.37 Ginnie Mae

Ginnie Mae means the Government National Mortgage Association administered by the United States Department of Housing and Urban Development, and its successors.

### 1.1.38 Governing Documents

Governing Documents means this Neighborhood Declaration, the Articles, Bylaws, Design Guidelines, Neighborhood Guidelines, Supplemental Neighborhood Declarations and Notices of Addition.

### 1.1.39 HUD

HUD means the United States Department of Housing and Urban Development.

### 1.1.40 Improvement

Improvement means any structure and any appurtenance thereto including a building, walkway, irrigation system, garage, road, parking area, fence, any type of wall, awning, walkway, stairs, balcony, any type of landscaping and planting, antenna, windbreak, the exterior surface of any visible structure and the paint on such surface, pole, sign, exterior air conditioning and water softener fixture or equipment. The Architectural Review Committee may identify additional items that are Improvements.

### 1.1.41 Include, Including

Whether capitalized or not, include and including means "includes without limitation" and "including without limitation," respectively.

### 1.1.42 Local Government Agency

Local Government Agency means the City, County, a public school district, a public water district, and any other local or municipal governmental entity or agency, including any special assessment district, maintenance district or community facilities district.

### 1.1.43 Maintain, Maintenance

Whether capitalized or not, maintain and maintenance mean "maintain, repair and replace" and "maintenance, repair and replacement," respectively; provided however, that maintain or maintenance shall not include repair and replace(ment) where the context or specific language of this Neighborhood Declaration provides another meaning.

### 1.1.44 Maintenance Guidelines; Maintenance Manual; Maintenance Recommendations

Maintenance Guidelines means any current written guidelines, setting forth procedures and standards for the maintenance and operation of Neighborhood Association Property by the Neighborhood Association and the Condominium by the Owners, that may be provided to the Neighborhood Association and to each Owner by Declarant, the Neighborhood Association or any governmental agency. Maintenance Guidelines include any Maintenance Manual initially prepared at Declarant's direction and containing recommended frequency of inspections and maintenance activities for components of the Neighborhood Association Property and any Maintenance Recommendations prepared by Declarant pertaining to a Condominium.

### 1.1.45 Manager

Manager means the Person retained by the Neighborhood Association to perform management functions of the Neighborhood Association as limited by the Governing Documents and the terms of the agreement between the Neighborhood Association and the Person.

### 1.1.46 Map

Map means the final recorded map of Tract No. 17756, as described in Exhibits A and B.

### 1.1.47 Master Declarant

Master Declarant means LENNAR CENTRAL PARK, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company.

### 1.1.48 Master Declaration

Master Declaration means the Master Declaration of Covenants, Conditions, Restrictions and Reservation of Easements for Central Park West, as currently in effect and as amended from time to time.

### 1.1.49 Member; Membership

Member means any Person holding a Membership and the Declarant. Membership means the voting and other rights, privileges, and duties established in the Governing Documents for Members of the Neighborhood Association.

### 1.1.50 Mortgage

Mortgage means any Recorded documents, including a deed of trust, by which a Condominium or other portion of the Neighborhood is hypothecated to secure performance of an obligation.

### 1.1.51 Mortgagee

Mortgagee means a Person to whom a Mortgage is made, or the assignee guarantor or insurer of the Mortgagee's rights under the Mortgage by a recorded instrument. For purposes of this Neighborhood Declaration, the term Mortgagee includes a beneficiary under a deed of trust.

### 1.1.52 Mortgagee Majority

Mortgagee Majority means the First Mortgagees holding First Mortgages that in the aggregate encumber at least fifty-one percent (51%) of the Condominiums that are subject to Mortgages. For purposes of any provisions of the Governing Documents which requires the vote or approval of a Mortgagee Majority, such vote or approval is determined based on one (1) vote for each Condominium encumbered by a First Mortgage held by a First Mortgagee.

### 1.1.53 Mortgagor

Mortgagor means a person who Mortgages his property to another. For purposes of this Neighborhood Declaration, the term Mortgagor shall include a trustor under a deed of trust.

### 1.1.54 Neighborhood

Neighborhood means (a) the Initial Covered Property and (b) each Phase described in a Notice of Addition. The Neighborhood is a "condominium project" as defined in California Civil Code Section 4125, and a "common interest development" as defined in California Civil Code Section 4100. Any references in this Neighborhood Declaration to the Neighborhood are references to the Neighborhood as a whole and to portions thereof. The Neighborhood constitutes a "Neighborhood" as defined in the Master Declaration.

### 1.1.55 Neighborhood Association

Neighborhood Association means Tribeca at Central Park West Association, a California nonprofit mutual benefit corporation (formed pursuant to the California Non-Profit Mutual Benefit Corporation Law or successor statutes), and its successors-in-interest. The Neighborhood Association is an "association" as defined in California Civil Code Section 4080.

### 1.1.56 Neighborhood Association Maintenance Funds

Neighborhood Association Maintenance Funds means the accounts created for Neighborhood Association receipts and disbursements pursuant to Article 7.

### 1.1.57 Neighborhood Association Property

Neighborhood Association Property means real or personal property designated by the Declarant as Neighborhood Association Property and therefore made subject to the restrictions on Neighborhood Association Property established in the Governing Documents. Neighborhood Association Property includes Improvements such as landscaping and irrigation, Private Streets and the residential building structures in the Neighborhood. Any references in this Neighborhood Declaration to Neighborhood Association Property are references to the Neighborhood Association Property as a whole and to portions thereof. Additional Neighborhood Association Property may be annexed to the Neighborhood pursuant to Article 16. In addition, Neighborhood Association Property includes all of the easements described in this Neighborhood Declaration and granted to the Neighborhood Association. The Neighborhood Association shall only have the responsibility for maintenance of such easements where such maintenance responsibility is given to the Neighborhood Association.

### 1.1.58 Neighborhood Declaration

Neighborhood Declaration means this instrument as currently in effect. This Neighborhood Declaration is a "Neighborhood Declaration" as defined in the Master Declaration.

### 1.1.59 Neighborhood Guidelines

Neighborhood Guidelines means the current rules and regulations for the Neighborhood.

### 1.1.60 Notice and Hearing

Notice and Hearing means written notice and a hearing before the Board, as provided in the Bylaws.

### 1.1.61 Notice of Addition

Notice of Addition means an instrument Recorded pursuant to Article 16 to annex additional real property to the Neighborhood. A Notice of Addition may include a Supplemental Neighborhood Declaration.

### 1.1.62 Official Records

Official Records means the Official Records of the County.

### 1.1.63 Operating Fund

Operating Fund means that portion of the Common Expenses allocated for the daily operation of the Neighborhood Association.

### 1.1.64 Owner

Owner means the Person or Persons, including Declarant, holding fee simple interest to a Condominium. Each Owner has a Membership in the Neighborhood Association. The term "Owner" includes sellers under executory contracts of sale but excludes Mortgagees. The term "Owner" may be expanded in a Supplemental Neighborhood Declaration to include other Persons.

### 1.1.65 Person

Person means a natural individual or any entity recognized under California law. When the word "person" is not capitalized, the word only refers to natural persons.

### 1.1.66 Phase

Phase means each of the following: (a) all the real property covered by this Neighborhood Declaration or a Notice of Addition for which a Public Report has been issued, and (b) real property consisting solely of Neighborhood Association Property as described in a Notice of Addition. Reconstruction Assessments are "special assessments" as described in California Civil Code Section 5600(a).

### 1.1.71 Record or File

### 1.1.72 Reserve Fund

Reserve Fund means that portion of the Common Expenses allocated for the future repair and replacement of, or additions to, structural elements, mechanical equipment and other major components of Neighborhood Association-maintained Improvements.

### 1.1.74 Rockefeller

Rockefeller means the condominium project located directly across the Private Streets from the Neighborhood on Lots 11 to 21, inclusive, of Tract No. 17756. Rockefeller is a "Neighborhood" as defined in the Master Declaration.

### 1.1.75 Rockefeller Association

Rockefeller Association means the Rockefeller at Central Park West Association, a California nonprofit mutual benefit corporation. Rockefeller is the homeowners association formed pursuant to the CID Act to operate and manager Rockefeller.

### 1.1.76 Special Assessment

Special Assessment means (a) a reasonable monetary penalty imposed against an Owner and the Owner's Condominium in accordance with California Civil Code Section 5725(b), as a disciplinary measure for the failure of an Owner to comply with the Governing Documents (but which may not be characterized nor treated in the Governing Documents as an Assessment that may become a lien enforceable by sale of the Condominium), or (b) a monetary charge imposed against an Owner and his Condominium in accordance with California Civil Code Section 5725(a) to recover costs incurred by the Neighborhood Association in the repair of damage to Neighborhood Association Property caused by the Owner or the Owner's Family, contractors, residents, tenants or guests (which may be characterized or treated as a lien enforceable by judicial foreclosure and sale of the Condominium), all as further described in the CID Act and this Neighborhood Declaration, (c) a Capital Improvement Assessment, and (d) an Assessment imposed to pay an extraordinary expense as defined in California Civil Code Section 5610.

### 1.1.77 Supplemental Neighborhood Declaration

Supplemental Neighborhood Declaration means an instrument Recorded by Declarant against all or a portion of the Neighborhood to supplement, modify, or clarify conditions, covenants, restrictions or easements established under this Neighborhood Declaration. A Supplemental Neighborhood Declaration may affect one or more Condominiums and Neighborhood Association Property, and it may annex additional real property to the coverage of the Neighborhood Declaration as long as it satisfies the requirements of a Notice of Addition in Article 16. Declarant may Record a Supplemental Neighborhood Declaration as long as Declarant owns all of the real property to be encumbered by the Supplemental Neighborhood Declaration. A Supplemental Neighborhood Declaration may modify this Neighborhood Declaration only as it applies to the property encumbered by the Supplemental Neighborhood Declaration.

### 1.1.78 Telecommunications Facilities

Telecommunications Facilities means Improvements constructed in the Neighborhood, including cables, conduits, ducts, vaults, connecting hardware, wires, poles, transmitters, towers, antennae and other devices now existing or that may be developed in the future to provide Telecommunication Services to the Neighborhood.

### 1.1.79 Telecommunications Services

Telecommunications Services means the reception, distribution or transmission of video, audio, data, telephony, all related vertical services, and any other similar services now existing or that may be developed in the future. Declarant may expand this definition in any Supplemental Neighborhood Declaration.

### 1.1.80 Unit

Unit means a separate interest in space as defined in California Civil Code Section 4125. Each Unit is a separate freehold estate, as separately shown, numbered and designated in the Condominium Plan. Each Unit includes a residential element and a garage element, the hardware on entry doors and garage doors, and the glass portions of the windows and sliding glass doors that are constructed at Unit boundaries. The boundaries of the Unit are approximately shown in the Condominium Plan. In interpreting deeds, the Neighborhood Declaration and the Condominium Plan, the actual boundaries of each Unit shall be deemed to extend to the unfinished surfaces of the interior walls, floors, and ceilings encompassing the residential element and garage element of the Unit, as constructed or reconstructed in substantial accordance with the original plans for the Unit. The foregoing interpretation shall apply notwithstanding any description expressed in the deed, the Condominium Plan or the Neighborhood Declaration, regardless of settling or lateral movements of Improvements, and regardless of minor variances between Unit boundaries, as shown in the Condominium Plan or defined in the deed and the Neighborhood Declaration, and the boundaries of the building Improvements as constructed or reconstructed.

### 1.1.82 VA Requirements

## 1.2 Interpretation

### 1.2.1 General Rules

This Neighborhood Declaration shall be liberally construed to effectuate its purpose of creating a uniform plan for creating and operating a residential condominium development and maintaining the Neighborhood Association Property. As used in this Neighborhood Declaration, the singular includes the plural and the plural the singular. The masculine, feminine and neuter each includes the other, unless the context dictates otherwise. Terms not defined herein shall have the meanings ascribed to them in the Master Declaration.

### 1.2.2 Articles, Sections and Exhibits

The Article and Section headings are inserted for convenience only and may not be considered in resolving questions of interpretation or construction. Unless otherwise indicated, any references in this Neighborhood Declaration to articles, sections or exhibits are to Articles, Sections and Exhibits of this Neighborhood Declaration. Exhibits attached to this Neighborhood Declaration are incorporated in this Neighborhood Declaration by this reference. The locations and dimensions of any Improvements shown on the Exhibits attached hereto and to any Notice of Addition are approximate only and the as-built location and dimension of any such Improvements shall control.

### 1.2.3 Priorities and Inconsistencies

If there are conflicts or inconsistencies between this Neighborhood Declaration and the Articles, Bylaws, Neighborhood Guidelines, a Supplemental Neighborhood Declaration or Condominium Plan, then the provisions of this Neighborhood Declaration shall prevail; however, the conflicting documents shall be construed to be consistent with the Neighborhood Declaration to the extent possible. If there are any conflicts or inconsistencies between this Neighborhood Declaration and the provisions of the Master Declaration or any Notice of Addition, the provisions of the Master Declaration or Notice of Addition shall prevail; however, such documents shall be construed to be consistent with one another to the extent possible.

### 1.2.4 Supplemental Neighborhood Declarations

As each Phase of the Neighborhood is developed, Declarant may, concerning that Phase, Record one (1) or more Supplemental Neighborhood Declarations, which shall designate the use classifications within the areas affected and which may supplement this Neighborhood Declaration with such additional covenants, conditions, restrictions and land uses as Declarant may deem appropriate for the real property being annexed thereby. The provisions of any Supplemental Neighborhood Declaration may impose such additional, different or more restrictive conditions, covenants, restrictions, land uses and limitations as Declarant may deem advisable, taking into account the particular requirements of each Phase. If there is any conflict between any Supplemental Neighborhood Declaration and the Neighborhood Declaration, the Supplemental Neighborhood Declaration shall control concerning the real property annexed by such Supplemental Neighborhood Declaration.

### 1.2.5 Severability

The provisions of this Neighborhood Declaration are independent and severable. A determination of invalidity, partial invalidity or unenforceability of any one (1) provision of this Neighborhood Declaration by a court of competent jurisdiction does not affect the validity or enforceability of any other provisions of this Neighborhood Declaration.

### 1.2.6 Statutory References

All references made in this Neighborhood Declaration to statutes are to those statutes as currently in effect or to subsequently enacted replacement statutes.

### 1.2.7 FHA Requirements

The FHA Requirements are incorporated herein by reference only if there are any FHA-insured Mortgages on Condominiums in the Neighborhood. While such FHA-insured Mortgages are in effect, the FHA Requirements shall control and prevail over all other provisions of the Neighborhood Declaration to the extent of any inconsistency between them.

### 1.2.8 VA Requirements

The VA Requirements are incorporated herein by reference only if there are any VA-guaranteed Mortgages on Condominiums in the Neighborhood. While such VA-guaranteed Mortgages are in effect, the VA Requirements shall control and prevail over all other provisions of the Neighborhood Declaration to the extent of any inconsistency between them.

## 1.3 Condominium Plans for Diagrammatic Purposes Only

Each Condominium Plan and the dimensions shown therein is intended to conform to California Civil Code Section 4285, which requires, in part, concerning the land and real property described therein, the inclusion of diagrammatic plans in sufficient detail to identify each Unit, its relative location and approximate dimensions. The dimensions shown in the Condominium Plans are not intended to be sufficiently accurate enough to use for sales purposes, or for computation of usable floor area or the volume of any portion of the Units or Exclusive Use Areas shown in the Condominium Plans. The diagrammatic plans contained therein intentionally omit information with respect to certain Improvements constructed within the Units. The actual dimensions and usable floor area or volume of a room, a Unit or Exclusive Use Area are all dependent on the Improvements as they were actually constructed, and there may be variances among Condominiums of the same floor plan. NONE OF THE DIMENSIONS DISCLOSED IN ANY CONDOMINIUM PLAN MAY BE RELIED UPON IN MAKING ANY DECISION TO PURCHASE, LEASE OR FINANCE A CONDOMINIUM. THEREFORE, ALL DECISIONS TO PURCHASE, LEASE OR FINANCE A CONDOMINIUM, TO THE EXTENT SUCH DECISIONS TAKE INTO ACCOUNT THE DIMENSIONS OR THE USABLE FLOOR AREA OR VOLUME OF ANY ROOM, UNIT OR EXCLUSIVE USE AREA SHOWN IN THE CONDOMINIUM PLAN, MUST BE BASED ONLY ON THE ACTUAL DIMENSIONS OF AS-BUILT IMPROVEMENTS AS DETERMINED BY PERSONAL ON-SITE INVESTIGATION.
