# Introduction

This page reproduces the introductory sections of the Neighborhood Guidelines, including the welcome, the purpose of the Association, cooperation, maintenance and inspection obligations, severability and the Code of Conduct.

Source: Neighborhood Guidelines (Residential Handbook), Tribeca at Central Park West Association, adopted by the Board July 24, 2018.

---

## Welcome to Tribeca!

Tribeca at Central Park West is a condominium neighborhood ("Tribeca" or "Neighborhood") in a unique urban location in the beautiful City of Irvine with a variety of amenities for the residents of the Neighborhood. Because attached living is a unique experience that relies on the mutual cooperation of all to be successful, Tribeca at Central Park West Association ("Neighborhood Association") created these Neighborhood Guidelines ("Guidelines") to be actively used as a reference guide for living at Tribeca. Inside you'll find practical rules, regulations and guidelines intended to help foster a harmonious, enjoyable and safe environment for all residents at Tribeca.

These Guidelines detail basic guidelines that, if observed, ensure that the structures and grounds of Tribeca remain in good condition and that neighbors treat each other with respect and consideration.

Bear in mind that the rules and guidelines established in these Guidelines are in addition to and supplement the Neighborhood Association's Neighborhood Declaration of Covenants, Conditions and Restrictions of Tribeca ("Neighborhood Declaration") and the Neighborhood Association's Articles of Incorporation and Bylaws. These documents (referred to collectively as "the Governing Documents") establish and govern the Neighborhood Association and Tribeca. The Board of Directors has the power to revise the rules, regulations, guidelines, policies and procedures set forth in these Guidelines from time to time.

As you read through these Guidelines, you will encounter defined terms, identifiable by their initial capital letters. Except as the context otherwise requires, these defined terms have the same meaning as set forth in the Neighborhood Declaration. Capitalized terms not defined herein are defined in the Governing Documents. In the event of a conflict between the Community Association Governing Documents and the Neighborhood Association Governing Documents, the Community Association provisions shall prevail.

Please read these Guidelines carefully, and be sure your family, guests and tenants fully understand and follow the rules, regulations and guidelines set forth below. If you have questions, please contact the Manager. To reach management, contact management through the homeowner portal.

## Tribeca at Central Park West Association

The purpose of the Neighborhood Association is to operate, manage and maintain Tribeca for the benefit of the residents. Common sense and consideration for your neighbors are keys to its success.

The Board governs the Neighborhood Association, and meets regularly to make decisions pertaining to those matters for which the Neighborhood Association is responsible. Owners will be notified of the date, time and location of all meetings of the Members and the Board. If you are interested in becoming involved in the Neighborhood Association, please contact the Manager.

Owners and residents of Tribeca are encouraged to work together to build a harmonious neighborhood. If any disputes between individual Owners should arise, the parties are encouraged to try to resolve them on their own.

To report problems related to the Neighborhood Association Property (such as landscape, parking area, lighting, noise, etc.), please contact the Manager.

## Cooperation

As a Condominium Neighborhood, Tribeca is a unique living environment that calls for mutual cooperation, common sense and consideration of neighbors. To facilitate harmony within Tribeca, all residents and their guests must comply with the rules and guidelines set forth in these Neighborhood Guidelines and the Governing Documents.

The Neighborhood Association welcomes communication from its Members. Please feel free to call or write to the Manager, the Neighborhood Association's liaison, to discuss any questions or issues, and the Manager will bring your questions or issues to the Board. Neighborhood Association Property shall herein be defined and referred to as "Association Property."

## Maintenance and Inspection Obligations

Owners, tenants and the Neighborhood Association have maintenance and inspection obligations. Owners should consult their Homeowner Reference Guide, applicable warranties and other manufacturers' maintenance schedules and recommendations for specific maintenance requirements. As set forth in the Neighborhood Declaration, a portion of the Owners' maintenance and inspection obligations require Owners to implement commonly accepted maintenance practices to prolong the life of the materials and construction of the Units. Similarly, specific maintenance and inspection requirements for the Neighborhood Association are set forth in the Neighborhood Association Maintenance Manual, applicable warranties and other manufacturers' maintenance schedules and recommendations.

## Severability

If any provision of these Guidelines is held to be invalid, the remainder of the provisions shall remain in full force and effect.

## Code of Conduct

All Owners, family members, tenants, their guests, visitors and other invitees are presumed to conduct themselves in a respectful and courteous manner with due consideration for each other as well as Neighborhood Association Board of Directors, vendors, Management, and their respective employees.

The use of obscene gestures, obscenities, verbal or physical threats or attacks directed at any Owner, resident, tenant, guest, visitor, invitee, a Board Member or Management will not be tolerated. Action by any person within the Neighborhood on any Neighborhood Association Property which may be dangerous or create a health or safety concern, a hostile environment, turmoil, disruption or disturbance among Owners, residents or others is not permitted.

An Owner or resident is responsible for any damage to the Neighborhood Association Property and/or non-compliance with the Governing Documents caused by or resulting from an Owner's or resident's own actions or the actions of family members, tenants, guests, service vendors, contractors and other invitees, consistent with the above paragraph.

Association Staff are to be treated in a courteous and considerate manner at all times. Association Staff shall not be reprimanded or harassed in any way by an Owner, resident, tenant, guest or other invitee. All complaints regarding service rendered by any Association Staff member must be made to the Association's General Manager or Board of Directors for appropriate handling.

The Association considers non-compliance with this Conduct Code by Owners or the Owner's tenants, residents, guests, invitees, agents and/or employees serious and will subject the responsible Owner to disciplinary action in accordance with the Association's Compliance Procedure.

In the event that an Owner fails to comply with this Conduct Code, Association Staff may, in their discretion, ask the Owner or guest to leave the premises and take whatever action is required to cause this action to occur.
