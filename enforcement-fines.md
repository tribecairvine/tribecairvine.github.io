# Enforcement & Fines

The Association's Enforcement Policy and Schedule of Fines is adopted under CC&Rs Article 12, Section 12.1.5, which authorizes the Board to adopt a reasonable schedule of fines and penalties, and procedures to address violations of the governing documents.

The specific fine amounts and the enforcement process (violation notice, hearing before the Board, and fines) are set out in the adopted policy. See [CC&Rs Article 12](ccrs-article-12.html).
