# Article I — Plan of Ownership

This article states the plan of ownership, defines how the Bylaws are interpreted, and sets the Neighborhood Association's name, application and treatment of statutory references.

Source: Bylaws of Tribeca at Central Park West Association.

---

## 1.1 Definitions and Interpretation

Unless otherwise provided in these Bylaws, the capitalized terms used in these Bylaws have the meanings they are given in the Neighborhood Declaration of Covenants, Conditions, Restrictions and Reservation of Easements for Tribeca at Central Park West (the "Neighborhood Declaration"), which was Recorded against the Neighborhood. These Bylaws shall be interpreted in accordance with Section 1.2 of the Neighborhood Declaration.

## 1.2 Name and Principal Office

The name of the Neighborhood Association is Tribeca at Central Park West Association. The principal office of the Neighborhood Association, if any, shall be located in the County.

## 1.3 Application

These Bylaws apply to the condominium development known as Tribeca at Central Park West, located in the County. All Persons who use the facilities of the Neighborhood in any manner, are subject to the regulations in these Bylaws and in the Neighborhood Declaration. Use of any Condominium in the Neighborhood signifies acceptance and ratification of these Bylaws.

## 1.4 Statutory and Regulatory References

All references made in these Bylaws to statutes or to regulations are to those statutes or regulations as currently in effect or to subsequently enacted replacement statutes or regulations.
