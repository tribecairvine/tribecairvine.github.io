# What to Do If You Are Stuck in an Elevator

Being stuck in an elevator is frightening but is usually not dangerous. Stay calm, stay inside, and use the built-in help button.

## Do this

1. Stay inside the cab. The elevator is designed to hold safely between floors.
2. Press and hold the emergency call button in the elevator. It connects to a 24-hour monitoring center.
3. Speak with the operator. They will confirm your location and dispatch help. Stay on the line if you can.
4. Wait calmly for help to arrive.

## Call 911 first if

- There is smoke, fire, or a medical emergency.
- Someone in the cab needs urgent help.

In a life-threatening situation, call 911 first, then use the elevator call button.

## Do not

- Do not force the doors open.
- Do not try to climb out of the cab or through the ceiling.
- Do not attempt to exit between floors.

## Reporting an elevator problem

If an elevator is out of service or behaving unusually and no one is trapped, contact management so it can be scheduled for service.
