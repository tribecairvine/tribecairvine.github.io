# Community Updates

Over the past year, the Board has worked to improve service quality, reduce unnecessary costs, and manage Association resources responsibly. This page is a high-level summary of major operational improvements.

Source: Board of Directors Transparency Report.

## Operational improvements

- **Elevator dispatch and telecom.** Separated elevator emergency dispatch from elevator maintenance for independent oversight, and consolidated redundant telecom and cellular accounts.
- **Lighting and electrical.** Moved to an electrical contractor with fair hourly rates, so routine work is billed appropriately and complex repairs receive proper attention.
- **Janitorial.** Restored water and drainage access and secured potable water service, improving cleaning efficiency and reliability, with transparent reporting.
- **Patrol and parking enforcement.** Restructured shared patrol so each community pays its fair share, while maintaining nightly patrol coverage. Patrol activity is tracked and reset quarterly.
- **Landscaping and irrigation.** Improved landscape service with weekly inspections, monthly reports, quarterly irrigation audits, and certified arborist oversight, plus smart irrigation controllers and a gradual move to low-maintenance, drought-tolerant plantings.
- **Cost-share enforcement.** Reviewing historical records so shared expenses, including patrol, irrigation, and utilities, are properly allocated among the communities going forward.
- **Reserves.** Moved reserve funds into an FDIC-insured CD and money-market mix to improve interest earnings while keeping liquidity for future projects.
- **Financial oversight.** Added dedicated oversight of vendor contracts, shared-cost verification, and general-ledger accuracy.

## Looking ahead

Continued focus includes deferred maintenance such as building exterior, paint, and asphalt, continued cost-share enforcement, and periodic financial transparency updates.
