# Article 14 — General Provisions

This article contains general provisions governing mergers, notices, and constructive notice and acceptance.

Source: Neighborhood Declaration of Covenants, Conditions, Restrictions and Reservation of Easements for Tribeca at Central Park West, recorded August 14, 2017 as Instrument 2017000341565, Orange County Official Records.

---

## 14.1 Mergers or Consolidations

In a merger or consolidation of the Neighborhood Association with another association, its properties, rights and obligations may, by operation of law, be transferred to another surviving or consolidated association or, alternatively, the properties, rights and obligations of another association may, by operation of law, be added to the properties, rights and obligations of the Neighborhood Association as a surviving corporation pursuant to a merger. The surviving or consolidated association may administer and enforce the covenants, conditions and restrictions established by this Neighborhood Declaration governing the Neighborhood, together with the covenants and restrictions established on any other property, as one (1) plan. Any such merger or consolidation requires the prior written approval of the VA.

## 14.2 No Public Right or Dedication

Nothing in this Neighborhood Declaration is a gift or dedication of all or any part of the Neighborhood to the public, or for any public use.

## 14.3 Notices

Except as otherwise provided in this Neighborhood Declaration, notice to be given to an Owner must be in writing and may be delivered personally to the Owner. Personal delivery of such notice to one (1) or more co-Owners, or any general partner of a partnership owning a Condominium, constitutes delivery to all Owners. Personal delivery of such notice to any officer or agent for the service of process on a corporation or limited liability company constitutes delivery to the corporation or limited liability company. Such notice may also be delivered by regular United States mail, postage prepaid, addressed to the Owner at the most recent address furnished by such Owner to the Neighborhood Association or, if no such address has been furnished, to the street address of such Owner's Condominium. Such notice is deemed delivered three (3) business days after the time of such mailing, except for notice of a meeting of Owners or of the Board, in which case the notice provisions of the Bylaws control. Any notice to be given to the Neighborhood Association may be delivered personally to any member of the Board, or sent by United States mail, postage prepaid, addressed to the Neighborhood Association at such address as may be fixed and circulated to all Owners.

## 14.4 Constructive Notice and Acceptance

Every Person who owns, occupies or acquires any right, title, estate or interest in or to any Condominium or other portion of the Neighborhood consents and agrees to every limit, restriction, easement, reservation, condition and covenant contained in this Neighborhood Declaration, whether or not any reference to these restrictions is in the instrument by which such Person acquired an interest in the Neighborhood. As soon as practicable before sale or transfer of title to a Condominium or other separate interest in the Neighborhood or execution of a real property sales contract therefor, the Owner of the Condominium or other separate interest shall provide to the purchaser copies of the Governing Documents listed in California Civil Code Section 4525 and its successor statutes.
