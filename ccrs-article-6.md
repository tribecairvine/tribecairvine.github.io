# Article 6 — Property Easements and Rights

This article reserves easements and rights of entry over the Neighborhood Association Property for the benefit of the Neighborhood Association, the Owners, Declarant, and others.

Source: Neighborhood Declaration of Covenants, Conditions, Restrictions and Reservation of Easements for Tribeca at Central Park West, recorded August 14, 2017 as Instrument 2017000341565, Orange County Official Records.

---

## 6.1 Easements

### 6.1.1 Maintenance and Repair

Declarant reserves for the benefit of the Neighborhood Association and all Neighborhood Association agents, officers and employees, nonexclusive easements over the Neighborhood Association Property as necessary to fulfill the obligations and perform the duties of the Neighborhood Association.

### 6.1.2 Utility Easements

Declarant reserves easements to install and maintain utilities over the Neighborhood Association Property for the benefit of the Owners and their Condominiums. Declarant reserves the right to grant additional easements and rights-of-way throughout the Neighborhood to utility companies and public agencies as it deems necessary for the proper development and disposal of the Neighborhood. Such right of Declarant shall expire on the Close of Escrow for the sale of the last Condominium in the Neighborhood and the Annexable Territory.

### 6.1.3 Community Association Easements

Declarant reserves for the benefit of the Community Association and all Community Association agents, officers and employees, nonexclusive easements over the Neighborhood Association Property for access, ingress and egress as reasonably necessary to fulfill their obligations and perform their duties; including without limitation the maintenance of Association Property (as defined in the Master Declaration) within or adjacent to the Neighborhood.

### 6.1.4 Cost Sharing Agreement

Declarant reserves for the benefit of the Rockefeller Association and the owners of homes within Rockefeller nonexclusive easements over the Private Streets and such other portions of the Neighborhood Association Property as reasonably necessary to access the mailbox clusters serving Rockefeller, all as described in the Cost Sharing Agreement.

### 6.1.5 Encroachments

Declarant reserves, for its benefit and for the benefit of the Owners, a reciprocal easement appurtenant to each Condominium over the other Condominiums and the Neighborhood Association Property to accommodate (a) any existing encroachment of any wall or any other Improvement installed by Declarant or approved by the Architectural Review Committee, (b) authorized construction or repair, and (c) shifting, movement or natural settling of the Residences or other Improvements. Use of these easements may not unreasonably interfere with each Owner's use and enjoyment of the burdened Residences.

### 6.1.6 Easements for Public Service Use

Declarant reserves easements over the Neighborhood for public services of the local government agencies, including but not limited to, the right of law enforcement and fire protection personnel to enter upon the Neighborhood to carry out their official duties.

### 6.1.7 Completion of Improvements

Declarant reserves the right and easement to enter the Neighborhood to complete any Improvement which Declarant considers desirable to implement Declarant's development plan.

### 6.1.8 Owners' Easements in Neighborhood Association Property

Declarant reserves, for the benefit of every Owner, and each Owner's Family, tenants and invitees, nonexclusive easements for pedestrian and vehicular access (all as applicable) over the Neighborhood Association Property in the Neighborhood as reasonably necessary for the use and enjoyment of each Condominium in the Neighborhood. This easement is appurtenant to and passes with title to every Condominium in the Neighborhood.

### 6.1.9 Public Access Easement

Declarant reserves for the benefit of the public a permanent nonexclusive easement over the paseos and other walkways in the Neighborhood for pedestrian access. The access easement reserved under this section may not be obstructed by any Improvement, including, but not limited to, gates, walls, or barriers of any kind, except for such walls and gates as may be necessary to protect the public from injury during repair and maintenance of such paseos and walkways. Notwithstanding any other provision of this Neighborhood Declaration, this section may not be amended without the express approval of Declarant.

### 6.1.10 Private Street Access Easements

Declarant reserves for the benefit of the Neighborhood Association, nonexclusive easements for pedestrian and vehicular access and parking of vehicles (the "Access Easements") over the improved portions of the Private Streets. Declarant shall maintain the Private Streets until they are conveyed in fee-simple title to the Neighborhood Association. The Access Easements shall become effective on a Phase-by-Phase basis when conveyed to the Neighborhood Association in an instrument Recorded with the first Close of Escrow in each Phase. The Access Easements shall be subject to temporary relocation or closure by Declarant to accommodate Declarant's construction activities, provided such relocation or closures does not prevent legal access from public streets to any Condominium then in the Neighborhood.

### 6.1.11 Exclusive Use Areas

Declarant reserves for the benefit of specified Owners exclusive easements over the Neighborhood for use of the Exclusive Use Areas, including, without limitation, for balconies and stairwells as described in this Neighborhood Declaration, any Notice of Addition or deed and described and shown on the Condominium Plan or Plans for the Neighborhood, and for internal and external telephone wiring, as originally installed and assigned to the Unit served thereby. Declarant also reserves, for the benefit of the Neighborhood Association, the right to enter the Exclusive Use Areas as necessary to perform the obligations of the Neighborhood Association. The foregoing easements shall be assigned by separate deed, and the easements so conveyed shall be appurtenant to and run with the Owner's Condominium.

### 6.1.12 Drainage Easements

Declarant reserves, for the benefit of the Neighborhood, the Owners and the Neighborhood Association, reciprocal nonexclusive easements for drainage of water over, across and on the Neighborhood.

## 6.2 Additional Easements

Declarant reserves easements over the Neighborhood Association Property owned in fee simple by the Neighborhood Association for the exclusive use by an Owner or Owners of contiguous property. Such conveyance must be approved by the Board, which approval must not be unreasonably withheld. The purpose of the easement, the portion of the Neighborhood Association Property affected, the Condominium to which the easement is appurtenant, and any restrictions on use of the easement area shall be identified in a Recorded grant of easement.

## 6.3 Delegation of Use

Any Owner may delegate his right to use the Neighborhood Association Property in writing to the Owner's tenants or contract purchasers who reside in such Owner's Residence, subject to regulation by the Board.

## 6.4 Right of Entry

### 6.4.1 Neighborhood Association

The Neighborhood Association has the right to enter the Exclusive Use Areas and the Condominiums to inspect the Neighborhood, and may take whatever corrective action it determines to be necessary or proper. Entry onto any Exclusive Use Areas or Condominium under this Subsection may be made after at least three (3) days' advance written notice to the Owner of the Condominium except for emergency situations, which shall not require notice. Any damage to a Unit or Exclusive Use Area caused by entry under this Subsection shall be repaired by the Neighborhood Association.

### 6.4.2 Declarant

The Declarant has the right to enter the Condominiums and the Neighborhood Association Property (a) to comply with requirements for the recordation of subdivision maps or lot line adjustments in the Neighborhood or Annexable Territory, (b) for repair of Improvements in accordance with the provisions of the Construction Claims Statute, (c) to accommodate grading or construction activities, and (d) to comply with requirements of applicable governmental agencies. Declarant shall provide the applicable Owner reasonable notice before such entry, except for emergency situations, which shall not require notice. Any damage to the Neighborhood that is caused by entry under this Subsection shall be repaired by the Declarant. Unless otherwise specified in the applicable initial grant deed by which Declarant has transferred ownership of the subject Condominium or subject Neighborhood Association Property, this right of entry shall automatically expire on the later of the date that is twelve (12) years after (a) the date this Neighborhood Declaration is Recorded, or (b) the date the grant deed is Recorded by which Declarant first conveyed fee title to the subject real property under authority of a Public Report.

### 6.4.3 Owners

Each Owner shall permit other Owners, and their representatives, to enter that Owner's Exclusive Use Area and Condominium to perform installations, alterations or repairs to the mechanical or electrical services to a Condominium if (a) requests for entry are made in advance, (b) entry is made at a time reasonably convenient to the Owner whose Exclusive Use Area or Condominium is to be entered; and (c) the entered Exclusive Use Area or Condominium is left in substantially the same condition as existed immediately preceding such entry. Any damage to the Exclusive Use Area or Condominium caused by entry under this Subsection shall be repaired by the entering Owner.
