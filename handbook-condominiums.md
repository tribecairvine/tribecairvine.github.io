# Condominiums ("Unit or Units")

This page reproduces the Neighborhood Guidelines rules that apply within and around the Units, including residential use, Exclusive Use Area balconies, alarms, noise, speakers, window coverings and cleaning, waterbeds, aquariums and resident-caused damage.

Source: Neighborhood Guidelines (Residential Handbook), Tribeca at Central Park West Association, adopted by the Board July 24, 2018.

The Neighborhood Guidelines established for the Neighborhood Association are intended to foster an environment of neighborliness, consideration and cooperation. These Neighborhood Guidelines constitute Neighborhood Association Rules contemplated by the Neighborhood Declaration. All Owners, residents and their guests are required to follow these Guidelines as a means of acting on behalf of the greater good of the community and its well-being. The Board has adopted these Guidelines, in addition to the provisions of the Neighborhood Declaration and the Bylaws.

Please refer to Article 8 of the Neighborhood Declaration for information regarding insurance requirements for the Neighborhood Association and Owner. If you have further questions, please contact the Manager or your insurance agent.

---

**1. Residential Use.** No Unit shall be occupied and used except for residential purposes by the Owners, their tenants, and guests. No trade or business shall be conducted in the Neighborhood. Refer to Article 2 of the Neighborhood Declaration for more information regarding the types of uses that are considered to be residential.

**2. Exclusive Use Area Balconies.** An Exclusive Use Area balcony is to be used as outdoor living areas containing furniture and other similar outdoor furnishings, equipped with protective leg caps or other devices to prevent damage to the floor surface. Balconies are subject to certain restrictions. Refer to Article 2 of the Neighborhood Declaration and the following additional restrictions:

**(a)** Owners or their tenants shall not change or alter the surface of any Exclusive Use Area balcony.

**(b)** The installation of any tiles or flooring material on the Exclusive Use Area balcony is strictly forbidden, as it will alter drainage patterns. Carpet, artificial turf or other material that can trap water next to the surface of the Exclusive Use Area balcony, is also prohibited. Owners and their tenants shall use all due care to prevent puncture of the water-proofing material on the Exclusive Use Area balconies.

**(c)** Existing drainage patterns on Exclusive Use Area balconies must be maintained and all drainage systems must be kept free of debris and free flowing. Changing the drainage pattern may cause damage to the buildings and structures.

**(d)** Potted plants must have a tray placed underneath the pot to prevent water spillage onto the Exclusive Use Area balcony. Such trays, and any other device designed to hold water, must be raised above the surface of the Exclusive Use Area balcony in order to allow sufficient air flow beneath such tray or device. No potted live plants are allowed on upper Exclusive Area balconies. When watering such plants, it must not be in a manner that causes water to encroach on Exclusive Use Area balcony appurtenant to other Units.

**(e)** Potted plants shall not be placed in a position on any Exclusive Use Area balconies which will block any drains or obstruct drainage patterns.

**(f)** No hanging screens, linens, blankets, rugs, towels, swimsuits, banners, wind chimes, plants or other objects may be hung from a Exclusive Use Area Balconies or railing.

**(g)** Residents shall not use any Exclusive Use Area balconies and stairwells for storage purposes, including, without limitation, the storage of bicycles, surfboards, barbeques, appliances and unapproved furniture.

**(h)** Residents shall use due care when cleaning their Exclusive Use Area balconies. Surfaces shall be mopped or swept clean in such a manner as to not cause any water to extend beyond the boundaries of the Exclusive Use Area balconies.

**(i)** No improvements shall be nailed, bolted, or otherwise attached to the floor, walls, or any portion of an Exclusive Use Area balconies.

**(j)** Any furniture, furnishings, or other materials kept on any Exclusive Use Area balcony shall be of a neutral color that is harmonious with the color scheme of the exterior walls of the building. Further, no Exclusive Use Area balcony furniture or any other items, including umbrellas, shades, shade structures, or cabanas may hang over the Exclusive Use Area balcony railing or be placed in a location that unreasonably interferes with the rights of another resident including obstructing another resident's view. Umbrellas, shade structures, and cabanas shall be sufficiently weighted so that they will not blow off the Exclusive Use Area balcony, but in no event shall an Exclusive Use Area balcony or Exclusive Use Area balcony railing be drilled, cut, pierced or modified in any manner.

**(k)** Heaters or fire pits are not allowed on Exclusive Use Area balconies.

**(l)** No vegetation shall extend beyond the railings, fences, walls and/or boundaries of an Exclusive Use Area balconies.

**(m)** Barbecues are not permitted on Exclusive Use Area balconies.

**(n)** No additional outdoor lighting may be installed on an Exclusive Use Area balconies.

**(p)** Enclosing any Exclusive Use Area balconies with netting or shades is prohibited.

**(q)** No antenna or satellite dish may be installed on an Exclusive Use Area balconies unless on a freestanding tripod. They cannot be attached to any balcony floor, railing, roof, wall or facia.

**(r)** No pets shall be left unattended on Exclusive Use Areas.

**3. Alarms.** Any alarm installed or connected in a Unit shall be the type of alarm which is monitored by a certified alarm company. Residents may not pierce any Party Wall, (separating one Unit from another or from the Neighborhood Association Property).

**4. Safety and Noise.** Please use common sense and courtesy in regard to voice levels, unnecessary noises and boisterous conduct. This includes, but is not limited to, televisions, surround sound systems, radios and/or other sound emitting devices. Keep the volume at a reasonable level at all times so other residents are not disturbed. AFTER 10:00 P.M., THE VOLUME MUST BE SIGNIFICANTLY REDUCED TO KEEP FROM DISTURBING OTHER RESIDENTS.

**5. Vibrations and Noise.** Residents shall not attach to the walls or ceilings of any Unit any fixtures or equipment which will cause vibrations or noise or unreasonable annoyance or damage to the residents of the other Units or to the Neighborhood Association Property.

**6. Speakers.** To help to ensure everyone's quiet enjoyment of their Unit, wall and ceiling mounted music, television or surround sound systems, including, but not limited to, wall mounted speakers or other audiovisual devices are not permitted to be supported by or come into contact with Party Walls. Speakers of any sort shall not be placed directly upon the floor. All speakers must be elevated from the floor by a proper acoustic platform, such as a speaker stand.

**7. Window Coverings and Treatment:** Subject to extension by the Board, Owners may cover their windows with white sheets for up to three (3) months prior to installing permanent window coverings. Permanent window coverings must have either a white or cream color backing or may be white or light-colored natural wood shutters. All window coverings shall be of a uniform neutral color harmonious with and not in conflict with the color scheme of the exterior wall surface of the building. Window tinting is not permitted.

**8. Window Cleaning.** Except for windows on Exclusive Use Area balconies, the Neighborhood Association will provide window cleaning services, once per year, for the exterior window surfaces that cannot be reached by residents. Residents must cooperate with the Neighborhood Association to provide access for such window cleaning. The Neighborhood Association will provide reasonable advance notice to residents of scheduled window cleaning dates.

**9. Waterbeds.** Waterbeds are not be permitted in any Unit. Each Owner and/or Owner's tenant acknowledges that substantial damage to other Unit and/or Neighborhood Association Property may occur as a result of a violation of this restriction and that the Owner or tenant causing such damage would be responsible for all damage.

**8. Aquariums.** Aquariums holding more than 30 gallons of water shall not be permitted in any Unit. Each Owner and/or tenant acknowledges that substantial damage to other Unit and/or Neighborhood Association Property may occur from a leaking or broken aquarium and that the Owner or tenant causing such damage would be responsible for all damage.

**10. Damage Caused by Owner or Residents.** Please take due care when using the Neighborhood Association Property. Owners may be liable to the Neighborhood Association for costs of repairs and/or replacement for any damage to the building, or any other Neighborhood Association Property, caused by such Owners, their residents, tenants/lessees, guests, employees or contractors.

## Editorial note on source numbering

The source document is reproduced as printed. In the list above, the item numbering runs 1 through 10 but the item titled "Aquariums" is printed as "8." (a second time, following "9. Waterbeds"), and the lettered sub-items under item 2 run (a) through (r) with no sub-item lettered "(o)". These are reproduced exactly as they appear in the recorded document and have not been renumbered.
