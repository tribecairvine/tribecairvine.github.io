# Exterior Cleaning: Power & Window Washing

Power washing begins Monday, August 17. This page is a reminder and a clarification of a few things going around the community.

## Please clear your hallway

Before your building's scheduled day, please remove personal items from the hallway outside your Unit. Shoes, mats, decor, planters, packages, anything that would sit in the vendor's path. Anything not removed cannot be cleaned around effectively and may get wet.

## Window washing is back and it is staying

Window washing has now resumed on a consistent annual schedule. That is a good thing and it is not going away.

There has been some confusion circulating about whether this program is connected to the construction defect matter. It is not. The two are completely separate. Routine exterior cleaning is standard operating maintenance. It has no impact on the construction defect case, and legal counsel has been consulted where appropriate.

For years, this service simply was not performed. The current board reinstated it. The goal now is consistency year over year, with steady improvements. Please treat any secondhand information you receive with skepticism and route questions to management directly.

## What can and cannot be washed

The Association's vendor cleans exterior glass residents cannot safely reach, plus interior hallways, stairwells, and ground-level common area stains.

The vendor does not clean interior glass, balcony sliding doors, screens, tracks, sills, or hard water spots. Those are Owner responsibility under CC&Rs Exhibit C.

If you want your windows cleaned by the vendor, remove your screens before your building's service date and reinstall after. The vendor is not contracted to handle screens.

## Costs, transparency, and the door is open

The full program is $11,900 for the year. Window washing is $7,000, power washing is $4,900. That works out to roughly $99 per Unit for the full annual exterior cleaning program.

This came in meaningfully below competitor quotes. If any resident believes they can source a qualified, insured, and Association-approvable vendor at a better price and scope, please send the proposal to management. The board reviews alternatives seriously at each contract cycle.

More broadly, volunteers are always welcome. If you want to help with vendor sourcing, walkthroughs, or committee work, please reach out to management and we will connect you.

## A note on the vendor

The Association's cleaning crew is on site to serve the community. Please be courteous to them. If you have a concern about the work, please route it to management rather than raising it with the crew directly.

## Questions and concerns

Please direct questions to management. Current management contact information is on your monthly assessment statement and in your homeowner portal.

On behalf of the Tribeca at Central Park West Board of Directors.
