# Article 16 — Annexation of Additional Property

This article sets out the methods by which additional real property may be annexed to the Neighborhood and brought under this Neighborhood Declaration.

Source: Neighborhood Declaration of Covenants, Conditions, Restrictions and Reservation of Easements for Tribeca at Central Park West, recorded August 14, 2017 as Instrument 2017000341565, Orange County Official Records.

---

Additional real property may be annexed to the Neighborhood and become subject to this Neighborhood Declaration by any of the following methods:

## 16.1 Additions by Declarant

Declarant may add the Annexable Territory to the Neighborhood and bring such added area under the general plan of this Neighborhood Declaration without the approval of the Neighborhood Association, the Board, or Owners, as long as Declarant owns any portion of the Annexable Territory. No amendment may be made to this Section 16.1 without the prior written approval of Declarant. Notwithstanding the foregoing, if FHA is insuring any Mortgages in the Neighborhood at the time the Annexable Territory is to be annexed, (a) Declarant shall obtain the prior written approval of FHA to such annexation, and (b) the quality of construction of Improvements in the Annexable Territory shall be consistent with that of the Improvements in the initial Phases, and (c) the right to add the Annexable Territory shall remain in effect until the later to occur of the seventh anniversary of the Recordation of the Neighborhood Declaration or the fifth anniversary of the Recordation of the latest Notice of Addition. Notwithstanding the foregoing, if VA is guaranteeing any Mortgages in the Neighborhood at the time the Annexable Territory is to be annexed, Declarant must obtain the prior written approval of VA for such annexation.

## 16.2 Other Additions

Additional real property may be annexed to the Neighborhood and brought under the general plan of this Neighborhood Declaration upon the approval by vote or written consent of Owners entitled to exercise no less than two-thirds (2/3) of the Neighborhood Association's voting power.

## 16.3 Added Territory Rights and Obligations

Subject to the provisions of Section 16.4, when a Notice of Addition containing the provisions required by Section 16.4 is Recorded, all provisions in this Neighborhood Declaration will apply to the real property described in such Notice of Addition (the "Added Territory") in the same manner as if the real property were originally covered by this Neighborhood Declaration. Thereafter, the rights, powers and responsibilities of the Owners, lessees and occupants of Condominiums in the Added Territory, as well as in the property originally subject to this Neighborhood Declaration, will be the same as if the Added Territory were originally covered by this Neighborhood Declaration. After the first day of the month following the first Close of Escrow in the Added Territory, the Owners of Condominiums in the Added Territory shall share in the payment of Assessments to the Neighborhood Association to meet Common Expenses of the Neighborhood. Voting rights attributable to the Condominiums in the Added Territory may not be exercised until Annual Assessments have commenced on such Condominiums.

## 16.4 Notice of Addition

The additions authorized under Sections 16.1 and 16.2 must be made by Recording a Notice of Addition against the real property to be added to the coverage of this Neighborhood Declaration. The Notice of Addition must (a) reference by instrument number this Neighborhood Declaration and the date of its Recordation, (b) describe with specificity the Added Territory, (c) state that this Neighborhood Declaration shall apply to the Added Territory, and (d) describe the land use designations in the Added Territory. The Notice of Addition for any addition under Section 16.1 must be signed by Declarant. The Notice of Addition for any addition under Section 16.2 must be signed by at least two (2) officers of the Neighborhood Association to certify that the Owner approval required under Section 16.2 was obtained. On Recordation of the Notice of Addition, the Added Territory will be annexed to and constitute a part of the Neighborhood and it will become subject to this Neighborhood Declaration. Subject to Section 16.3, the Owners of Condominiums in the Added Territory will automatically acquire Membership in the Neighborhood Association. No Notice of Addition or Supplemental Neighborhood Declaration may revoke the covenants, conditions, restrictions, reservation of easements, or equitable servitudes in this Neighborhood Declaration as the same pertain to the real property originally covered by this Neighborhood Declaration.

## 16.5 De-Annexation and Amendment

In addition to the rights to amend or terminate a Notice of Addition granted elsewhere in this Neighborhood Declaration or in a Notice of Addition, Declarant may also amend a Notice of Addition or delete all or a portion of a Phase from coverage of this Neighborhood Declaration and the Neighborhood Association's jurisdiction so long as Declarant is the owner of all of such Phase and provided that (a) an amending instrument or a Notice of Deletion of Territory, as applicable, is Recorded in the same manner as the applicable Notice of Addition was Recorded, (b) Declarant has not exercised any Neighborhood Association vote concerning any portion of such Phase, (c) Assessments have not yet commenced concerning any portion of such Phase, (d) Close of Escrow has not occurred for the sale of any Condominium in such Phase, and (e) the Neighborhood Association has not made any expenditures or incurred any obligations concerning any portion of such Phase. No amendment may be made to this Section 16.5 without the prior written approval of Declarant.
