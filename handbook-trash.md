# Trash Storage and Disposal

This page reproduces the Neighborhood Guidelines rules governing storage and disposal of trash.

Source: Neighborhood Guidelines (Residential Handbook), Tribeca at Central Park West Association, adopted by the Board July 24, 2018.

---

The trash containers for a Unit shall be stored in the Unit's garage, and may only be set out at the curbside directly behind the Unit or the Unit's garage door for a reasonable period of time for trash collection purposes (not to exceed twelve (12) hours before and after scheduled trash collection hours). The trash containers for a Unit may not be set out at the curbside behind any other Unit. An Owner and other occupants of a Unit may be required to move the Unit's trash containers as necessary on trash collection days for purposes of ingress to and egress from the Unit's garage, and that Unit's trash containers shall not be placed behind another Unit (including another Unit's garage) except for the limited purpose of facilitating ingress to or egress from the Unit's garage, and then only for such limited time as necessary to facilitate such ingress or egress. Failure to strictly abide by Section 2.17 of the Declaration may result in disciplinary action against a Unit's Owner by the Board. Furthermore, in no event shall such trash containers be stored in any Exclusive Use Area of any Unit. Trash containers owned by the Neighborhood Association may be kept on Neighborhood Association Property so long as they are contained in an enclosure installed by Declarant or approved by the Design Review Committee (DRC).

Oversized items should not be left outside. It is the resident's sole responsibility to remove these items from the premises and dispose of them at their discretion. Owners should contact the refuse hauler to make special arrangements for removal of these items.
