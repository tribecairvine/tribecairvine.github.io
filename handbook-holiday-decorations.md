# Holiday Decorations

This page reproduces the Neighborhood Guidelines rules governing holiday decorations and lighting.

Source: Neighborhood Guidelines (Residential Handbook), Tribeca at Central Park West Association, adopted by the Board July 24, 2018.

---

**1. Reasonable Timeframes.** Outdoor decorative/holiday lights and/or decorations, or indoor holiday decorations that are visible from outside can be installed seven (7) days prior to the holiday and must be removed within fourteen (14) days after such holiday, unless written authorization has been granted by the Association. Owners shall not pierce the exterior surface of the building for the purpose of displaying holiday decorations, or for any other purpose.

**2. Location.** No resident may place holiday decorations in the Neighborhood Association Property. Residents may not damage or puncture the Neighborhood Association Property or any other portion of the building in the process of displaying decorations.

**3. Lights.** Outdoor holiday lighting is permitted on Exclusive Use Area balconies, as long as no nails or tacks penetrate any part of a building, eve or facia.

**4. Christmas Trees and Greenery.** Christmas trees and greenery must be transported to and from a Unit in a bag to avoid any needles from dropping on Neighborhood Association Property.
