# Elevator Use

This page reproduces the Neighborhood Guidelines rules governing use of the elevators.

Source: Neighborhood Guidelines (Residential Handbook), Tribeca at Central Park West Association, adopted by the Board July 24, 2018.

---

Please do not tamper with the elevator stop switches. The elevators have been electronically programmed to provide the most efficient service under normal conditions. Holding open elevator doors, pressing order buttons and corridor buttons unnecessarily will cause the service to be less efficient.

Do not block or hold the elevator doors open for a prolonged periods of time.

If the elevator stops for no apparent reason and stalls, use the phone provided in the elevator to notify the elevator monitoring company. Emergency personnel will come as soon as possible to let you out.
