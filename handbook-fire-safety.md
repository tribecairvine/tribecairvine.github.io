# Fire Safety Devices

This page reproduces the Neighborhood Guidelines rules governing smoke and carbon monoxide detectors, fire sprinklers, obstruction of common areas and MERV filters.

Source: Neighborhood Guidelines (Residential Handbook), Tribeca at Central Park West Association, adopted by the Board July 24, 2018.

---

**1. Smoke and Carbon Monoxide Detectors.** Each Owner must maintain the smoke and carbon monoxide detectors, alarms and horns installed in his or her Unit. As part of this maintenance, Owners must replace all detector batteries regularly, if applicable.

**2. Fire Sprinklers.** Each Owner and their tenants must take care not to harm, damage or unnecessarily activate the fire sprinklers installed in Owner's Unit. The fire sprinklers are heat activated and permitting high heat, steam or burning in the vicinity of a fire sprinkler may cause it to activate, potentially causing extensive damage to your Unit, your personal property, the Neighborhood Association Property, and the Units adjacent to or below yours. Except for periodic dusting, you should never touch or allow anything else to touch the fire sprinklers. Nothing should be stored within eighteen (18) inches of a sprinkler head. Sprinkler heads are not to be painted. Painted sprinkler heads will require replacement by the Neighborhood Association at the Owner's expense.

**3. No Obstruction.** Obstruction of the corridors, lobbies, or entranceways throughout Tribeca is not permitted. The Neighborhood Association will not be responsible for any damage to, or loss of, any personal property left in any Neighborhood Association Property.

**4. MERV Filters.** The Units in the Neighborhood contain Minimum Efficiency Reporting Value 14 ("MERV 14") filters within the intake of the Unit's ventilation system. Each Owner of a Unit with a MERV 14 filter shall maintain the MERV 14 filter at all times in accordance with the manufacturer's recommendations, any Maintenance Guidelines provided by Declarant, and any Maintenance Guidelines provided by the Neighborhood Association.
