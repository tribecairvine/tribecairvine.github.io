# Parking & Garage Rules

These rules govern parking, vehicle registration, and garage use for residents, owners, and visitors.

Source: Parking and Garage Rules & Regulations for the Tribeca and Rockefeller Neighborhoods at Central Park West, version "Updated July 18, 2026." Final adopted May 12, 2021, with later revisions noted at the end of this page.

---

## Definitions

- **Sub-Association** - Any condominium association or other Owners association also known here as "Neighborhood(s)" created pursuant to the provisions of a Supplemental Declaration and having concurrent jurisdiction with the Association over the portion of the Development added by such Supplemental Declaration. Rockefeller at Central Park West is a Sub-Association of The Central Park West Community Association.
- **Master Association** - Organization that is authorized to exercise some or all of the powers of one or more associations on behalf of one or more common interest communities or for the benefit of the unit Owners of one or more common interest communities. The Central Park West Community Association is the Master for Rockefeller at Central Park West.
- **Open Parking Space(s)** - Open and available parking spaces are located throughout both Tribeca and Rockefeller. These open parking spaces are outside of enclosed garage parking designated for Owners.
- **Common Area(s)** - Common Area means the volumes of airspace in the Condominium Plan for each Phase, which shall be owned by the Owners of the Units in the Phase as tenant-in-common.
- **Alleyway(s)** - The narrow street between the buildings.
- **Owner(s)** - Owner means the Person or Persons, including Declarant, holding fee simple interest to a condominium. Each Owner has a Membership in the Neighborhood Association. The term "Owner" includes sellers under executory contracts of sale but excludes Mortgagees. The term "Owner" may be expanded in a Supplemental Neighborhood Declaration to include other Persons.
- **Resident(s)** - A homeowner on title or a renter or tenant who is residing in a homeowner's unit.
- **Visitor(s)** - Any Individual(s) who are visiting a homeowner and do not reside in a unit or community.
- **Safelist** - Represents a single approved overnight stay for a single vehicle on a single night or multiple nights.
- **Authorized Vehicle(s)** - The following vehicles are "Authorized Vehicles:" (a) standard passenger vehicles, including automobiles, vehicles designed to accommodate ten (10) or fewer people, (b) motorcycles and (c) pick-up trucks having a payload capacity of one (1) ton or less.
- **Prohibited Vehicle(s)** - The following vehicles are "Prohibited Vehicles": (a) recreational vehicles (including motorhomes, travel trailers, camper vans, jet skis, motor boats and other motorized vehicles designed for travel over water), (b) commercial-type vehicles (including pick-up trucks having a payload capacity in excess of one (1) ton, stake bed trucks, tank trucks, dump trucks, step vans, concrete trucks, limousines, vehicles with commercial signage and other commercial purpose vehicles) or vehicles with advertisements, placards or other writing on the vehicle or any equipment on a vehicle used for any commercial purpose, (c) vehicles designed to accommodate more than ten (10) people, (d) vehicles having more than two (2) axles, (e) trailers, inoperable vehicles or parts of vehicles, (f) other similar vehicles, or (g) any vehicle or vehicular equipment deemed a nuisance by the Board.

## General Restrictions

- All Owners must register their vehicles by providing a completed Homeowner Vehicle Information Form (HVIF) immediately upon move-in. If the Unit is occupied by Residents other than the Owners on title including tenants, a completed HVIF must be provided including their information. Any subsequent changes to vehicle information will also require an updated form be submitted.
- All Authorized vehicles owned or operated by or under the control of an Owner or a Resident of an Owner's Unit and kept in the Properties must be parked in the garage of that Unit at all times until capacity is reached.
- All open spaces or street parking on Tribeca and Bowery are for Visitors and Guests only and not for Residents or Owners.
- Visitor parking is limited and there may not be adequate parking to serve all guests of the Tribeca or Rockefeller Neighborhoods.
- Visitor parking must be Safelisted for overnight parking from 10:00 pm to 6:00 am. Regardless of overnight Safelisting, open space parking is designated solely for Visitors and Guests at all times.
- Each Unit will be allotted 15 overnight Safelists per quarter (every four three months).
- Vehicles may not be parked in open spaces for more than 72 consecutive hours.
- Residents who necessitate their guests to park for more than 72 hours will need prior written approval from the Association in addition to being Safelisted.
- No Resident or Visitor may park a vehicle in a manner which the Neighborhood Association determines either restricts the access or passage of pedestrians or vehicles over garage areas, alleyways, driveways, streets or sidewalks in the Neighborhood. Any Vehicle restricting access or passage may be towed without prior warning or citation.
- No parking is permitted in fire lanes as designated by posted signage or within 15 feet of a fire hydrant. Parking in these areas will result in an immediate tow without prior warning or citation.
- No maintenance or restoration of any vehicle may be conducted on the Properties except in a garage only when the garage door is closed.
- Washing of vehicles is not permitted in alleyways, open parking spaces or any other Common Areas in the Tribeca and Rockefeller Neighborhoods or Central Park West Community Association.
- Open Space Parking may include parking designated solely for Tribeca or solely for Rockefeller Visitors and Guests or for both jointly Tribeca and Rockefeller Visitors and Guests. The Tribeca and Rockefeller Neighborhoods' Map will specify these designations further along with signage and/or stenciled markings near the Open Spaces.
- All parking outside of the Tribeca and Bowery Sub-Association streets are monitored and patrolled by The Central Park West Community Association and other neighboring Sub-Associations. The specific parking guidelines for the neighboring Sub-Associations and Central Park West Community Association, known as the Master, can be confirmed by calling [phone number redacted].
- Residential garages shall be solely used for parking purposes and shall be used to park the number of Authorized Vehicles the garage was originally designed to accommodate.
- Residents may not operate a business out of any portion of the Property including the garage.
- Residents possessing fewer vehicles than the number for which the garage was originally designed may use any additional area in the garage for storage or recreational activities;
- Residents may not, under any circumstances, use any portion of the garage as a temporary or permanent living space for people or animals of any kind, including but not limited to, dogs, cats, rodents (e.g., rats, mice and hamsters), rabbits and reptiles (e.g., snakes and lizards).
- Garage doors shall be kept closed at all times when not in use.
- Owners, Residents and their Visitors whose vehicles leak oil and stain or cause other damage to the Common Area streets and/or open parking spaces may be subject to fines and/or an assessment to pay the cost of cleanup and repair.
- Vehicles that would otherwise be considered prohibited may be authorized by the Tribeca and Rockefeller Board of Directors and parked or kept in the Common Area or open parking spaces in connection with management or maintenance.
- Vehicles that would otherwise be considered prohibited may be authorized by the Tribeca and Rockefeller Board of Directors for brief periods for purposes of loading, unloading, making deliveries or emergency repairs and are to use the designated loading and unloading areas as indicated by yellow hash markings within the Neighborhoods.
- Prohibited Vehicles may be parked in an Owner's garage only if, and to the extent that, an Owner has fewer Authorized Vehicles than the number of vehicles the garage was originally designed to hold.
- Vehicles parked in open spaces marked as handicap parking must have a disabled placard displayed.

## Enforcement

Notwithstanding the foregoing, no vehicle shall be towed for violations of these Rules unless it has received three (3) or more documented violations within a rolling twelve (12) month period, and at least seventy-two (72) hours have elapsed since the third violation. Upon the third violation, the patrol vendor shall report the vehicle to the Board of Directors and Management, who shall attempt outreach to the associated Owner prior to authorizing any tow. This paragraph does not apply to vehicles subject to immediate tow under these Rules for restricting access, fire lane parking, or fire hydrant zone violations.

Any vehicle (including vehicles belonging to any members of the Resident's family, guests, Visitors, contractors, vendors or similar) that is found to be in violation of these Neighborhood Rules and the Neighborhood Declaration, is subject to towing, fines as permitted by law, and any other disciplinary action the Board may determine is appropriate, including requiring verification of adherence to these Neighborhood Rules and Neighborhood Declaration as further defined in the Association's Enforcement and Fine Policy and Post Orders. Should the Neighborhood Associations receive complaints or evidence of garages not being used for their intended purpose of parking authorized vehicles and until capacity is reached, the Associations shall have the right to visually inspect the interior of all garages to ensure compliance of the Rules.
